# Eyes and gaze

Use the accepted neutral face and front/oblique/side/underside views. Identify iris, pupil, sclera, highlight, and lid line separately. Record gaze range, expressions, existing controls, UVs, and target renderer. Check globe curvature, opening, and rotation center. A painted pupil does not imply a physical hole; a cornea or iris depression is an explicit design choice.

Keep lid details attached to the lids. Diagnose gaze axes/centers separately from painted placement. Test blinks, winks, wide eyes, and required combinations; inspect sclera leaks, inner-wall exposure, intersections, and return. Verify unaffected brows, mouth, face material, save/reopen, and export. Engine appearance remains a separate check.
