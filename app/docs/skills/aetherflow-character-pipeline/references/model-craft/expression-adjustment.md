# Local expression edits

Start from an accepted neutral face and matched views. Separate basis shape, folds, painted lines, normals, and exposed inner surfaces. Do not transfer vertex deltas from unrelated topology. For blinking, define inner/center/outer lid landmarks in the local eye frame and move the rim plus neighboring rows coherently, fading through the surrounding skin. Keep brows fixed unless included.

For eye folds, inspect whether adjacent rows rotate on incompatible axes; change the faulty area rather than the whole eye. For mouth-only edits, inspect corner depth separately from frontal width and height, protecting jaw and outline. Test neutral, intermediate values, required combinations, shadows, dependent lines, and return. Historical ratios are examples, not defaults.
