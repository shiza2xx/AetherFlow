# Layered garments and hidden support

Inspect each garment layer alone, including hidden body and underside. Classify missing/reversed/duplicate faces, unintended cross-layer joins, UV/normal seams, and intentional openings separately. Do not cap a free hem or close a limb path by default.

Prioritize visible penetration over minor shading. Distinguish cloth penetration from a limb leaving a free edge. Apply coherent corrections with the waist/support fixed or fading; independent pushes can invert layer order. Check combined motion for stretch and new intersections. Update hidden support correspondence only where needed. Test required walk, leg raise, and seated motion, reporting protrusion, layer inversion, stretch, and continuity separately.
