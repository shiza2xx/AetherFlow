# Multi-angle shape consistency

Choose one accepted illustration as the identity reference. Compare front, oblique, and true side views; add underside only when needed to understand volume. Match crown, chin, facial landmarks, ears, neck, skull depth, and cheek/jaw volume. Keep expression, scale, and projection consistent. Treat obscured construction as uncertain until supported by a view.

Keep the head upright and neutral. Do not flatten volume to match one perspective or invent disconnected profiles. Inspect coarse geometry in plain shading before expression work. Additional explanatory angles are optional; required production files remain independent views rather than mirrored or collaged substitutes.
